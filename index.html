Research Interests

Multimodal Learning for Predictive Physical Interaction Understanding
I develop multimodal models that understand how agents interact with objects and reason about the physical consequences of their actions. Building on my work in temporal action analysis, vision–language retrieval, and domain generalization, I aim to learn transferable representations of motion, object states, and interaction context from multimodal observations — with the ultimate goal of anticipating future interactions and action outcomes to support reliable decision-making in dynamic, real-world environments.

What I've Worked On

  • Multimodal Human Proficiency Estimation (SkillLens) — Action-guided Prototype Dynamics Margin (PDM) and CORAL-based ordinal calibration for proficiency estimation; 54.2% accuracy on Ego-Exo4D (+4.1pp over the strongest evaluated baseline). CVPR 2026 Workshop on MOMA (Oral). [project page]
  • Robust Text-to-Image Person Re-Identification (ongoing, KIST) — Text-based person retrieval for missing-person and suspect searches where only natural-language descriptions are available.
  • Federated Domain Generalization for Person Re-Identification (KIST) — Identity-ranking diagnostics and leave-one-client-out analysis for a deployed CCTV missing-person search system scaling across heterogeneous municipal environments.
  • Pose-Robust Representation Learning for Person Re-Identification (SwapMixer) (KIST) — Generation-free feature-level augmentation combining identity representations with pose embeddings from different identities.
  • Knowledge Distillation for Open-Vocabulary Object Detection (Yonsei B.S. capstone) — Similarity-preserving distillation aligning teacher–student relational representations; improved novel-class box AP50 on COCO by +1.2 points (23.1 → 24.3).
